How the challenges work

  1. Open the module you are studying and pick the next challenge in order.
  2. Read the challenge brief — its README.md — starting with What You Will Practice.
  3. Build your own solution from the brief alone, in your own folder.
  4. Only when you have finished, open the reference page, compare structure with structure, and then read its source.
