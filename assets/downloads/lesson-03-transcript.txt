ACCESSIBLE WEB MEDIA - LESSON 3
Captions, transcripts and audio description
Instructor: Lina Moreau
Running time of the lesson clip: 5 seconds

TRANSCRIPT OF THE SAMPLE CLIP
The sample clip has no spoken audio. It is a five second time-lapse: a closed
pink flower bud fills the frame, then the petals unfold until the flower is
fully open.

WHY A SILENT CLIP STILL NEEDS TEXT
A viewer who cannot hear the audio needs to know that there is nothing to
hear; silence with no caption is indistinguishable from a broken caption
track. A viewer who cannot see the video needs a description of what happens,
because nothing in the audio conveys it.

WHAT TO PRODUCE FOR EVERY CLIP
1. A caption track for everything audible, including "[no dialogue]".
2. A text description track, or an audio description, for everything visible
   that matters.
3. A full transcript on the page or linked from it, like this file.
