Questions people ask before buying

  • What counts as a survey?

    One completed form, filed. Drafts, revisions and photographs attached to a filed survey do not count again, however many times you edit them.

  • What happens if we go over the limit?

    Nothing stops working. We tell you in the app, and if you are over for two months running we email about moving up a plan. We never charge an overage fee without asking first.

  • Can we move between plans?

    Any time, in the app, and it takes effect immediately. Moving down is pro-rated; we refund the difference rather than crediting it.

  • Do you offer a charity or education price?

    Half price on every plan for registered charities, schools and universities. Email us from an institutional address and we apply it the same day.

  • What happens to our data if we leave?

    You can export everything at any time, and the export includes the photographs at full resolution. After you cancel we keep the data exportable for 90 days, then delete it.
