What Signal measures

Six things, properly. Everything else is somebody else's product.

  • Funnels that survive a redesign

    Steps are defined by what the user achieved, not by which button they pressed, so renaming a class or moving a screen does not silently break six months of history. When a step does break, Signal tells you rather than quietly reporting zero.

  • Live alerting Running now

    Watch a release in real time and get told when a step's completion rate moves outside its normal range.

  • Shareable answers

    Every chart has a link that shows the same numbers to somebody without an account, with the query attached.

  • Privacy that survives legal review

    No cookies, no cross-site identifiers, EU hosting by default, and a deletion request completes in minutes.

  • A snippet that costs 4 KB

    One script, no dependencies, no layout shift, and it fails silently if our servers are unreachable.

  • Your data, exportable

    Raw events out as CSV or Parquet, on a schedule, without asking us and without an enterprise plan.
