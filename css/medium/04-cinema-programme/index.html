The Quiet Tide

Certificate 12A 1 h 52 m Directed by Maren Sølvberg

Elin has skippered the family boat for eleven years. When the quota is withdrawn, her father wants to fight it, her daughter wants to sell, and Elin has six weeks to decide which of them she is going to disappoint. Norwegian and English with English subtitles.

Screening times

North of Here

Certificate 15 2 h 06 m Directed by Idris Halloran

A road film that never leaves one county. Two estranged brothers drive a decommissioned ambulance from Carlisle to the coast to scatter their mother's ashes, and get as far as an argument about a petrol station in 1998.

Screening times

No captioned screening this week; the film returns captioned on 6 September.

Salt and Stone

Certificate PG 1 h 28 m Directed by Priya Venkataraman

A documentary about the last working salt pans in the Camargue, shot over four seasons with no narration and a great deal of patience. Our Sunday matinee is the relaxed screening: lights up, sound down, come and go as you like.

Screening times

Tue 16:00 Thu 16:30 Sat 11:00 Sun 11:30

Thursday 16:30 is audio described. Sunday 11:30 is our relaxed screening.
