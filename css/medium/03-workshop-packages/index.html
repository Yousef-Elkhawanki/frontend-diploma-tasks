Service packages

Three packages cover almost everything a bike needs. If yours needs something else, we will tell you what and what it costs before we start — we have never once phoned a customer mid-job to add to a bill.

£45 Same day, if it is in by 10:00

Tune-up

For a bike that is basically fine and feels slightly wrong.

  • Gears and brakes adjusted and cable tension checked
  • Wheels trued in the frame
  • Tyres, chain wear and bolt torque checked
  • Drivetrain wiped down and lubricated

About 45 minutes on the bench. Parts are extra and quoted first.

£95 Two to three working days

Full service

For a bike ridden all year, once a year.

  • Everything in the tune-up
  • Drivetrain removed, degreased and refitted
  • Wheels off, hubs checked and adjusted
  • Headset and bottom bracket checked and greased
  • Brake pads and rotors cleaned or replaced
  • Frame washed and inspected for cracks

Our most booked package. Book a week ahead in March and April.

£180 Five working days

Overhaul — our most thorough package

For a bike that has been in a shed since 2019, or a bike you intend to keep for twenty years.

  • Frame stripped to bare metal parts
  • Every bearing cleaned, inspected, regreased or replaced
  • Wheels rebuilt if the rims are good, replaced if not
  • New cables, housing, chain and bar tape included
  • Full geometry and fit check with you on the bike
  • Twelve-month adjustment plan, free of charge

Includes the parts listed. Anything else is quoted before we order it.

Booking is by telephone or in person only, because half of the job is a conversation about how the bike feels. Ring 0113 296 0780 or drop in.
