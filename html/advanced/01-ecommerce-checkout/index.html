Help, returns and warranty

Can I return it if it is the wrong size?

Yes. Sit on it for up to 60 days; if the size is wrong we collect it free of charge and either exchange it or refund you in full. We do not ask for the box back.

What does the ten-year warranty cover?

Every moving part, including the gas lift, the mesh and the armrest mechanism. It covers normal office use for up to 12 hours a day. It does not cover a chair used as a stepladder, which we see more often than you would think.

Are spare parts really available for ten years?

Yes, and they are listed publicly with prices. A castor is £4, a mesh panel £22, a gas lift £38, a complete armrest £29. Our oldest supported chair is from 2016 and we still stock every part for it.

How hard is assembly?

Four bolts and about ten minutes, with the tool included. The assembly service is for people who would rather not, not for people who cannot manage it.

What happens to my old chair?

Our driver will take it away free of charge if you ask when you order. Anything repairable goes to a refurbisher in Bradford; anything else is stripped for materials. We will tell you which happened to yours if you want to know.

Do you sell to businesses?

Yes, from five chairs upwards, with net-30 invoicing and a VAT invoice on request. Call 0113 496 0100 or email business@northlight.example.
