LISBON IN THREE DAYS - SUGGESTED ITINERARY
Prepared by Tejo Travel for the Alfama & Belem package

DAY 1 - ALFAMA AND THE OLD CITY
09:30  Walk up to Miradouro das Portas do Sol (free)
11:00  Sao Jorge Castle, guided tour included in your package
13:00  Lunch at Tasca do Chico, Rua dos Remedios
15:30  Fado museum
20:00  Fado evening, included on Tuesday and Friday departures

DAY 2 - BELEM AND THE RIVER
09:00  Tram 15E from Praca da Figueira
10:00  Jeronimos Monastery, timed entry 10:15
12:30  Pasteis de Belem
14:00  Ferry across the Tejo to Cacilhas
17:00  Sunset at Cristo Rei viewpoint

DAY 3 - SINTRA DAY TRIP
08:26  Train from Rossio station (40 minutes, ticket included)
10:00  Pena Palace, timed entry 10:30
13:00  Lunch in Sintra village
15:30  Quinta da Regaleira
18:00  Return train to Lisbon

WHAT TO BRING
Comfortable shoes with grip - the Alfama pavements are steep and polished.
A refillable water bottle; public fountains are marked on the city map.
