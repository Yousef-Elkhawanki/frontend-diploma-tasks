Questions buyers ask

Can I use them while they charge?

Yes, over USB-C or 3.5 mm. Charging while playing adds about 40 minutes to a full charge.

Do the ear pads really come off without tools?

They twist off by hand. The headband needs a coin or a flat screwdriver to release two clips. Replacement pads are £19 a pair.

Will they work with my flight entertainment system?

Yes, with the 3.5 mm cable. Two-pin airline sockets need an adapter, which is not included.

What happens if I break them outside the warranty?

We publish the spare parts list and the service manual, and our workshop in Leeds quotes for repairs on any Aurora One regardless of age. We have never refused a repair on age alone.
